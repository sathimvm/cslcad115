Platform Overview

ShipView3D is a specialized, high-performance visualization platform engineered to meet the rigorous demands of maritime architecture and dimension control. Designed as a seamless, web-based CAD viewer, it bridges the gap between complex 3D ship models and active field operations. By providing precise sectioning, axis-locked millimeter measurements, and intelligent multi-touch interactions, the application ensures structural accuracy and spatial awareness whether used on a desktop workstation or a mobile tablet directly on the shipyard floor.

Developer

Satheesh Raghavan
Hull Dimension Control Team
Cochin Shipyard Ltd.

Inspired by the principles of operational excellence and technological integration I experienced during my AOTS training in Japan, I developed this platform to streamline our dimension control workflows and bring precise, professional-grade CAD visualization directly to the hands of our shipyard teams.
